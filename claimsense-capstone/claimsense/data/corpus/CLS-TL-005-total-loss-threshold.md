# CLS-TL-005 — Total loss determination

A vehicle is a total-loss candidate when the reasonable cost of repair equals or
exceeds seventy-five percent (75%) of the vehicle's actual cash value (ACV)
immediately before the loss. Total-loss candidates must be referred to a licensed
total-loss appraiser for valuation before any repair authorization. Settlement on a
total loss is ACV less the applicable deductible and salvage where retained.
