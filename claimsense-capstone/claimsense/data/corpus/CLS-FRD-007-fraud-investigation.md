# CLS-FRD-007 — Fraud investigation and referral

The insurer may refer any claim presenting indicators of misrepresentation or a
staged loss to the Special Investigation Unit (SIU) before payment. Indicators
include, without limitation: unwitnessed late-night single-vehicle losses, absence
of a police report where injury or airbag deployment is reported, repair estimates
inconsistent with the described damage, and multiple prior claims within twelve
months. SIU referral suspends repair authorization until the investigation resolves.
