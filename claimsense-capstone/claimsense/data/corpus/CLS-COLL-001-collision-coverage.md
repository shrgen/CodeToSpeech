# CLS-COLL-001 — Collision coverage — insuring agreement

Under Full Coverage policies, the insurer pays for direct and accidental loss to the
covered vehicle caused by collision with another vehicle or object, or by upset of the
vehicle, regardless of fault. Payment is limited to the lesser of the reasonable cost
of repair or the actual cash value (ACV) of the vehicle immediately before the loss,
reduced by the applicable collision deductible shown on the declarations page.
